## 🌐 Option 1: Netlify Drop (Instant Live Web Hosting in 30 Seconds)

1. Open **[app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)** in your browser.
2. Drag and drop your **`Avelour_Bloomora`** folder from your Desktop into the netlify upload box.
3. ✨ **Done!** Your site will instantly go live on a URL like `https://avelour-bloomora.netlify.app`.
4. (Optional) Connect your custom domain `bloomora.global` in Domain Settings.

---

## 🐙 Option 2: GitHub Repository & GitHub Pages (Free Hosting)

1. Go to **[github.com/new](https://github.com/new)** and sign in to GitHub.
2. Enter Repository Name: `avelour-bloomora` (Set visibility to **Public**).
3. Click **Create repository**.
4. On the new page, click the link: **"uploading an existing file"**.
5. Drag and drop all files from `C:\Users\hp\Desktop\Avelour_Bloomora` into GitHub.
6. Click **Commit changes**.
7. Go to **Settings &rarr; Pages** in your GitHub repository.
8. Under **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and click **Save**.
9. ✨ Your website is now live on `https://<your-username>.github.io/avelour-bloomora/`!

---

## ⚡ Option 3: Vercel Hosting

1. Go to **[vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new)**.
2. Drag & drop the `Avelour_Bloomora` folder to deploy in 1-click!
